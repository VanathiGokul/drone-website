# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Enterprise executives, research partners, technical directors, and commercial/industrial stakeholders evaluating autonomous systems (aerial drones and autonomous mobile robots / AMRs) for industrial operations, surveillance, and automated logistics.

## Product Purpose
Showcase Cavin Infotech's autonomous robotics capabilities across Air (Drones) & Ground (AMR), highlighting engineering scopes, research breakthroughs, hardware-software integration, and operational outcomes to drive executive briefing requests and commercial partnerships.

## Positioning
Dual-domain autonomous innovation combining high-payload aerial robotics with intelligent terrestrial AMRs, backed by edge AI companion computing, ROS2 navigation stacks, and precision sensor fusion.

## Operating Context
High-impact interactive web showcase viewed on modern desktop and mobile browsers, designed for fast scanning by leadership and deep inspection by technical evaluators.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Framework: React 18 with Vite and Tailwind CSS.
- Design Authority: The approved Figma design (accessible via the connected Figma MCP) is the definitive source of truth for all visual components, layouts, typography, spacing, and styling. No unsolicited redesign or deviation from the Figma spec.
- Code integrity: Preserve existing component logic and content while aligning styling directly to Figma specs.

## Brand Commitments
- Brand: Cavin Infotech.
- Typography: Orbitron (headings / display metrics) and Space Grotesk (body / technical descriptions).
- Theme: High-tech dark theme (`#0A0A0B`) with precision industrial accent colors (Amber/Orange `#EC8922`, Cyan `#00F0FF`, and subtle grid / glassmorphism overlays).
- Assets: Official Cavin Infotech branding and robotics graphics.

## Evidence on Hand
- Approved Figma workspace and components accessed via Figma MCP.
- Visual mockup reference (`drone_website_design.png`).
- Existing React components in `src/components/`.

## Product Principles
1. **Figma as Truth**: Treat the approved Figma design as the authoritative blueprint for visual hierarchy, layout, styling tokens, and interaction states.
2. **Precision & Craft**: Reflect the rigorous engineering standards of aerospace and autonomous ground robotics in visual alignment, typography, and contrast.
3. **Executive & Technical Resonance**: Balance high-level executive storytelling (impact, outcomes, CTA) with rigorous technical credibility (specs, architectures, research data).
